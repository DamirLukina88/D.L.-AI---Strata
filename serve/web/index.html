Speech Recognition in Brave Browser
Local Whisper STT is available!

Strata has 100% local, offline Whisper speech recognition installed (ggml-base.bin). You can record voice directly in Brave without sending audio to Google or any cloud server.

Why Cloud Speech-to-Text is restricted in Brave:

Brave disables Google's proprietary cloud speech recognition servers by default to prevent transmitting your voice recordings to third parties. Unlike Google Chrome, current versions of Brave do not include cloud STT endpoints.

Alternative Options:
  • Mobile / Android Brave: Use the microphone icon directly on your virtual keyboard (Gboard / Samsung Keyboard) to dictate into the input field.
  • Desktop Voice Typing: Local Whisper (recommended above) or your operating system's native voice typing (e.g. OS Dictation).
  • Brave Flags Configured: System Speech-Dispatcher (for TTS audio playback) and on-device speech flags have been configured in ~/.config/brave-flags.conf. Restart Brave for flags to take effect.
